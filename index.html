About the Awards

A New Era of Street Culture. Built by Passion.

APEX Motor Awards isn't just a car show — it’s a celebration of the builders, drivers, and engineers shaping automotive culture. We’re here to spotlight late nights in the garage, roaring engines, and fearless custom builds. We reward engineering mastery, bold aesthetics, and pure speed — the true ingredients of a legend.

Selection Criteria

JUDGING CRITERIA

  • ENGINEERING

    Technical complexity of modifications, component reliability, and build quality.

  • AESTHETICS

    Body kit harmony, wheel fitment, paint quality, and attention to detail.

  • INNOVATION

    Out-of-the-box technical solutions and the use of modern materials.

  • PERFORMANCE

    Real track numbers, telemetry data, and handling dynamics.

  • IMPACT

    Audience response, project recognition, and contribution to the community.
